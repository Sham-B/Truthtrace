Help & Glossary

How TruthTrace works, plain-language terminology guide, and user role specifications.

How TruthTrace Validates an Application (6 Steps)

1️⃣

Upload Papers

College uploads statutory affidavits, land deeds, NOCs, and TER receipts.

2️⃣

Document Check

OCR extracts structured fields and cross-checks them against AICTE master records.

3️⃣

Tamper Check

Forensic scans detect spliced text, high-frequency noise disparities, and PDF metadata edits.

4️⃣

Issuer Check

Ed25519 digital signatures in QR seals authenticate directly against issuing authority ledgers.

5️⃣

Advisory Report

Triangulation scores confidence and concern, presenting plain English next steps.

6️⃣

Officer Order

Human scrutiny officer enters a reasoned legal decision, permanently hash-chained into history.

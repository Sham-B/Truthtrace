## ⏱️ Second-by-Second Presentation Script (120 Seconds)

### 🎬 Part 1: The Hook & The Problem (0:00 – 0:20 | 20s)
* **Starting Screen:** `Home` Dashboard (`#navHome`).
* **Physical Action:** Stand tall, speak with energy, point your cursor at the live dashboard stat cards and donut chart.

> **🗣️ What to Say (Verbatim):**  
> *"Judges, every year regulatory bodies like AICTE process thousands of institutional approvals manually. Existing AI tools fail here because they act as black boxes offering a single naive 'fraud score' that no court or scrutiny officer can legally defend.*  
> *Meet **TruthTrace**—the first explainable regulatory verification platform governed by the principle: **AI Only Advises; Scrutiny Officers Legally Decide**.*  
> *Instead of one black-box score, we evaluate every application across **three independent checks**: Document Compliance, Forensic Tampering, and Cryptographic Provenance."*

## 🎯 Judges' Toughest Q&A (10-Second Killer Responses)

* **Q: "Can LLMs hallucinate a rejection or approve a fraudulent college?"**
  * **Answer:** *"Impossible by design. TruthTrace enforces **Advisory-Only Governance**. LLMs only generate draft explanatory summaries. Adverse regulatory decisions require an authenticated Scrutiny Officer entering a legally reasoned justification, permanently sealed into our SHA-256 audit log."*

* **Q: "What if a fraudster deepfakes a clean document with Photoshop or AI?"**
  * **Answer:** *"Photoshop may fool the human eye, but it cannot forge our **Cryptographic Provenance Lane**. Every genuine certificate embeds an Ed25519 digital signature seal. If even a single digit of the survey number or intake capacity is modified, the signature fails mathematically."*

* **Q: "What if the document is genuine but the college reused it from another campus?"**
  * **Answer:** *"That's our **Cross-Application Reuse Ring Engine**. We extract normalized claims across all submitted applications and index them in a shared contradiction graph, immediately flagging whenever land deeds, trust PANs, or faculty IDs overlap across institutions."*

* **Q: "Does this require expensive cloud GPUs?"**
  * **Answer:** *"Zero GPUs required. TruthTrace is engineered with a **100% CPU-optimized footprint** (~220 MB RAM) using PyMuPDF, OpenCV, and mathematical frequency roll-off, allowing deployment on standard government desktop workstations."*
